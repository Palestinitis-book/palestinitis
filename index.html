Preface

A short opening to set the tone of Palestinitis — quiet, observant, and direct.

I'm not a writer. I've never written a book before. Now I wish I had because I desperately want to write this book, and I don't have the skill to do so. It's worse for you, the reader, who is now trying to suffer through this. Like listening to a toddler who is learning to play the violin.

But this isn't for you, dear reader. I am writing this for my children, and only for them. On day, dear daughters, you might ask me - what happened? And I won't be ready to answer you. My mind will explode with all the madness that occurred over these last long two years. And my tongue will fail me while my mouth angrily vomits out the first heinous thing I can remember from this time. I will have failed you.

So I'm going to write it down. Just for you. And when you come to learn about this period in history. Which is your history as Jews, as Israelis (and you are neither but it is your history all the same) - you will at least have one voice speaking about it that you can trust. I cannot lie to you. It would not help you. I wrote something in my notes about tigers in a forest…

The voice a father to his daughters can only say things that he believes. I have no desire to mislead my children, for it could only harm them. I want them to know the truth. No “my truth” but an objective truth. To know the world as it exists. A truth that will be of use to them. There is no point believing there are tigers in the forest if there are none, and neither is there use in believe there are none when there are.

Having said this, I'm going to be wrong about a whole load of stuff here in this book. I haven't even written a single page but I know I will make mistakes, trust the wrong source, interpret sources poorly, and a plethora of other pitfalls will trap me that would not trap a skilled or experience writer or journalist. Again, I am neither. But I'll do my best. Approach with an open mind, with scepticism, with a questioning attitude.

And read plenty of other sources. And approach them critically too. You can at least trust that I am trying to lead you to the truth. It's a warzone out there.
